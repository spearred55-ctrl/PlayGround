HOW TO SURVIVE

TOUCH

Left joystick moves. Drag the right side to look. Fire, aim, jump, crouch and reload with the right-side buttons. Use PACK to manage loot and MAP for the island.

KEYBOARD

WASD move · Mouse look · LMB fire · RMB aim · R reload · 1/2/3 weapons · Space jump · C crouch · Shift sprint · E interact · Tab pack · M map.

THE ZONE

The Aurora wall closes in phases. Outside it you take damage. Watch the timer and rotate early. The blue ring is the current safe zone; the amber ring shows where it will close next.

LOOT

Weapons, ammo, armor, helmets, backpacks and medical supplies spawn at landmarks. Rarer weapons handle better. Armor absorbs damage; helmets reduce headshot damage.

VEHICLES

Find the ridge buggy near roads and landmarks. Press interact to drive, steer with the joystick, and exit before the vehicle takes too much damage.

VICTORY

Be the last operative or team standing. Eliminated players can spectate the remaining match. Practice mode is a smaller, faster bot arena.
